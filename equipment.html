Important conditions

  1. Use equipment according to its instructions and under appropriate medical-professional guidance. It is not a substitute for medical advice or emergency care.
  2. Return equipment by the agreed date in the condition received, allowing for ordinary wear and tear. Report damage or faults promptly.
  3. The standard maximum loan period is 30 days, subject to availability and any written extension approved by the organization.
  4. Provide accurate information, use the equipment only for its intended purpose, and do not transfer, sell, or misuse it.

Requests are subject to availability, identity/eligibility checks where applicable, and the organization's equipment handover process. The organization will explain the deposit and refund terms before handover.
