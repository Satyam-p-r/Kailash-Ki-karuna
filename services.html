Service conditions

An ambulance request is subject to availability and confirmation. Arrival may be delayed or the service may not be possible due to circumstances such as vehicle availability, traffic, weather or other operational reasons. Our team will communicate the status as soon as possible. Do not delay contacting emergency services while waiting for a response.

Transportation is subject to availability, route conditions and confirmation by our team. Delays or inability to complete the journey may occur due to circumstances beyond our control. The family or requester is responsible for required documents and applicable permissions.
