These proposed terms describe the general process for requesting home-care medical equipment from Kailash ki Karuna. The final approved policy, including the applicable deposit and refund procedures, should be published here before accepting requests.

1. Availability and requests

Submitting a request does not reserve equipment or guarantee availability. A request becomes an approved loan only after the organization confirms availability, applicable terms, and the handover arrangements.

2. Security deposit and refund

The organization will communicate the applicable refundable security deposit, payment method, refund process, expected timeline, and any permitted deductions before the equipment is handed over. No deposit amount or refund timeline should be assumed until confirmed.

3. Duration and return

The standard maximum loan period is 30 calendar days, unless an extension is approved in writing. The borrower must return the equipment by the agreed date and follow the organization's return instructions.

4. Safe and appropriate use

Equipment must be used for its intended purpose and according to the manufacturer's instructions and appropriate medical guidance. The equipment does not replace professional medical advice or emergency services.

5. Care, damage and faults

Borrowers should take reasonable care of the equipment, report faults or damage promptly, and avoid unauthorized repairs, modifications, transfer, or resale. The organization will explain how damage, ordinary wear and tear, and any applicable deductions are assessed before handover.

6. Accurate information and eligibility

Requesters must provide accurate contact and delivery information. The organization may request reasonable information needed to determine eligibility and arrange the equipment loan, in accordance with its privacy policy and applicable law.

7. Responsibility and safety

Borrowers must follow safety instructions and seek appropriate professional guidance for medical use. Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability where such exclusion or limitation is prohibited by applicable law, including any liability arising from negligence where it cannot lawfully be excluded.

8. Privacy

Information provided in a request should be used only for the stated operational purposes and handled according to the organization's privacy policy and applicable law.

9. Questions and disputes

For questions about an equipment request, deposit, return, or these terms, please contact the organization using its verified contact details. Applicable legal rights remain unaffected.
